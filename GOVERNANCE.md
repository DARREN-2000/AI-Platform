# Project Governance

This document describes the governance structure for the Enterprise AI Platform.

## Roles

*   **Users:** Anyone who interacts with the platform.
*   **Contributors:** Anyone who submits code, documentation, or other artifacts.
*   **Maintainers:** Individuals with commit access who are responsible for reviewing and merging PRs.
*   **Steering Committee:** A subset of maintainers responsible for the overall strategic direction of the project.

## Decision Making

We strive for consensus. If consensus cannot be reached, the Steering Committee will make the final decision.
